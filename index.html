Our story

We didn’t come to make another energy drink.

We came to rethink what one could be.

  1. 01

    Flavour first

    Every can starts with fruit — tart cranberry, sun-ripe mango, sharp lemon — tuned until the first sip lands loud.

  2. 02

    Nothing to hide behind

    No sugar and no caffeine means the flavour has to do the talking. So we made it bold.

  3. 03

    A smarter boost

    B vitamins and electrolytes in a light, 8-calorie can. A different kind of energy drink.

Made for real life

From your first plans to your last adventure.

XS is made for grown-up days that ask a lot of you — at a desk, in the gym, on the trail.

  • Active lifestyles

    Bike rides, park sessions, the long way home. Cold, bright and ready when you are.

  • Everyday refreshment

    Desk, commute, the mid-afternoon lull. Big flavour, without the sugar.

  • Adult fitness

    Post-class, post-run, post-PB. Electrolytes, B vitamins and 8 kcal a can.

  • Family outings

    Park days and picnic blankets — one for the grown-ups’ cool box.

  • Weekend adventures

    Trailheads, beaches and road trips. One for the road.

XS is designed for adults. Not recommended for children.
